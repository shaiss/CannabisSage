# CannabisSage roadmap

Status: Living roadmap. Edit by PR; product owner is the PM.

## North star

Help shoppers understand listed cannabinoids and terpenes, compare products on the page, and find close chem matches across nearby supported menus—in plain chem terms.

## Guardrails

- No medical or effects claims. Talk in listed chemistry only.
- No remote code. The extension ships its own logic; the server may send data and config, not executable code.
- The shopper decides. CannabisSage proposes and explains; it never places orders.

## Phases

### 1. Market share (now)

Grow installs and everyday use of chem on supported menus. Free covers hover badges, compare, similar picks on the same menu, and product-page chem. Pro adds taste-map match, filters and sort, export, deal and $/mg badges, and chem insights across more supported stores. Ship each piece when it is solid.

### 2. Profile and availability

A saved taste profile, picks that match it across nearby supported menus, and restock and deal alerts—so shoppers can see what is listed now without checking every menu by hand.

### 3. Trust layer

Verified lab results from partners, so listed chemistry is easier to trust than the menu alone. Verified status stays proof-based.

### 4. Ask Sage

A chat guide that explains listed chemistry in plain words, grounded in the shopper’s profile and the menus we support. Answers cite the products and numbers they come from.

### 5. Voice, avatar, and grower side

Voice in and out, then an optional avatar, plus later B2B and partner work for growers and menus. Verified status and ranking can never be bought.
